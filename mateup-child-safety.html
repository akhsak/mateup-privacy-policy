MateUp Child Safety Standards

MateUp is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Zero Tolerance

MateUp strictly prohibits child sexual abuse, child sexual exploitation, child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, and any other exploitation or abuse of minors.

Reporting

Users can report inappropriate or unsafe content or behaviour through the reporting and support mechanisms provided by MateUp.

Action Against Violations

Accounts or content that violate our child safety standards may be investigated, restricted, suspended, or removed.

Contact

For child safety concerns, please contact: samad@samsoftwares.com

MateUp is committed to complying with applicable child safety laws and regulations.
